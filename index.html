PRIVACY POLICY

1. Privacy & Data Minimization

Users interact with the application through anonymous identifiers. We do not collect, store, or track personal user information such as names, email addresses, or account profiles on our servers.

2. Data Collected Locally

To ensure proper functionality and a smooth user experience, the app stores certain preferences and profile details locally on your device (such as your display mode, language preferences, and personal profile inputs like gender, height, weight or body shape). These settings are saved solely on your device. You can permanently delete all local data at any time using the "Reset Data" option.

3. Images and Uploaded Content

Personal images are not permanently stored on our servers and are deleted immediately after the session is complete.

4. Temporarily Generated Content

For generation content, these images are not permanently stored on our servers and are automatically deleted from our third-party providers' infrastructure within a maximum timeframe of 30 days.

5. Payments and In-App Purchases

Your credits purchase are handled securely and directly by the App Store. We do not store or have access to your credit card or banking details.

6. Advertising and Third-Party Networks

The app displays advertisements provided by third-party ad networks. These partners may automatically collect non-personal technical data (such as device advertising identifiers or IP addresses) to serve, personalize, and measure advertisements.
